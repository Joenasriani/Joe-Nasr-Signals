Creative Direction & Advertising

Creative direction and production across advertising, branded content, campaigns, visual communication and multimedia work for international and regional brands. Examples include Chopard, Mercedes-Benz and Dubai Culture and Arts Authority.

Design & Visual Communication

Graphic design, branding, typography, visual systems, campaign design, retouching and communication-focused creative work. Examples include Maybelline New York, Estée Lauder and Sephora.

Film, Video & Post Production

Editing, post-production, compositing, visual finishing, video production and work across broader audiovisual production pipelines. Examples include Etihad Airways, E! News and Pepsi Arabia.

Motion Graphics, Animation & 3D

Motion graphics, animation and 3D work across branded, editorial and experimental visual projects. Examples include Adidas, B.Games and NYX Professional Makeup.

Media Music Composition

Interactive & Technology

Interactive Experiences, XR & Games

Design and development of browser experiences, interactive prototypes, games, WebXR and VR projects, spatial interfaces and experimental digital systems. Examples include RoboSim, Brick XR Builder, Diorama and Ascension. Quest ReQuest VR is an independent Meta Quest content and community ecosystem.

AI & Creative Technology

Development and exploration of AI-assisted systems, reasoning tools, visual frameworks, experimental interfaces and creative-technology prototypes. Examples include Visual Framework App, Roundtable AI and DealFindr AI Compass.

Product & Venture Development

Documented venture and product activity spanning concept development, positioning, design, prototyping and public release. Examples include RoboMarket, Apex Innovate and Daoversal.

Knowledge & Training

Corporate Training & Guest Lecturing

Professional training and guest lecturing across design, multimedia and creative technology, including corporate, academic and government engagements. Examples include Government of Dubai Media Office (GDMO), Dubai Police and American University in Dubai (AUD).

Research, Reasoning & Publishing

Independent work across reasoning, decision structures, human behavior, research frameworks and publishing. Publications include Manipulation: The Fool and the Wise, The Structure of Life: The Structure of Reasoning and ICF-AI.
