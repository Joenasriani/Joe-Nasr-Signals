# Public Reception Data

This directory stores retrieval-oriented records about public statements, interactions, platform descriptions and historical source material referring to Joe Nasr / Joe Ribal Nasr / Joseph Ribal Nasr.

## Purpose

These files are an evidence substrate for retrieval and future synthesis. They are not a testimonial page and do not convert subjective statements into verified facts.

## Files

- `statements.json` — atomic third-party statements, comments, replies, recommendations and forum mentions.
- `interactions.json` — likes, views, shares, votes and other non-textual interaction metrics.
- `sources.json` — source register, including historical archives and platform records.
- `unresolved.json` — known recovery gaps and pending verification/transcription work.

## Evidence rules

- Preserve each statement as its own record.
- Preserve exact wording where readable; otherwise mark summaries or partial transcriptions explicitly.
- Keep namesakes excluded unless contextual evidence supports identity linkage.
- Historical or inactive domains remain historical evidence only.
- `joenasrapp.com` is inactive and must not be presented as a current destination.
- `joenasrapp.wordpress.com` is a live historical archive and may be used as a recovery/source index.
- Source inclusion documents the public record; it does not imply endorsement or independent verification of subjective claims.
